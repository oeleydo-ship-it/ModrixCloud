# Asset provenance

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for this theme. No reference-site logos or product photographs are bundled. All images use an opaque white or pale marble background.

The hero brief requested a wide pale marble tabletop, a silver perforated portable speaker and an unbranded phone on the right, with clear space on the left for editable HTML text. The primary VR image brief requested an ivory headset with four sensors, black cushion, pale straps and two controllers in a centered studio photograph. Phone-coral and speaker-teal use the same white-background product-photography direction as the remaining catalog. Gallery side/back views were generated as edits of the primary VR image to preserve the product.

The recorded prompts for the subsequent catalog assets and gallery edits, along with the original generated-image locations, are in image-prompts.json. Original PNGs remain at those locations; shipped WebP copies were resized and encoded for the storefront. Work Sans is covered by OFL-WorkSans.txt.
