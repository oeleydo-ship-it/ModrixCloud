# Artwork provenance

Original Sable Atelier campaign and catalog artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool for this theme. No reference-site logos, product artwork or brand copy are bundled. PNG masters remain in the generation output folder; optimized JPEG derivatives are shipped here. The catalog master was divided into twelve individual product tiles.

## Prompt set

Hero: Wide landscape 3:1 editorial fashion photograph for original unbranded travel accessories. Dramatic sunlit sandstone canyon desert, stylish female traveler in an olive coat holding a plain olive woven travel tote, right of center. Warm cinematic natural photography, large scenic empty space on left for overlay typography. No text, no logos, no monograms.

Studio: Wide landscape 3:1 premium product still life: original unbranded chocolate woven carryall tote, caramel leather weekender and ivory zipper pouch on stone plinth in a warm walnut studio, sculptural side lighting. Editorial photography. No text, no logos, no monograms.

Editorial: Wide landscape 3:1 fashion editorial photograph: traveler in dark charcoal coat carrying ivory canvas weekender standing on rocky plateau, majestic blue gray mountains in background. Cinematic desaturated natural lighting, traveler positioned right of center, open landscape left. No text or logos.

Catalog: Contact sheet twelve original unbranded luxury travel products arranged precisely in a FOUR COLUMN by THREE ROW grid, each cell identical dimensions, no gutters, uniform light gray studio backgrounds. Each product fully visible with margins, centered. Row1 charcoal hard shell carryon suitcase, olive canvas weekender bag, sand woven tote bag, chocolate leather zipper pouch. Row2 slate blue woven tote, ivory crossbody bag, mahogany leather bucket bag, moss canvas backpack. Row3 ivory hard shell carryon, blush organizer pouch, chestnut leather shoulder bag, charcoal vanity case. No writing no logos no monograms no labels. Landscape 4:3 image.

## Transparent product derivatives

The original twelve-product contact sheet was edited with the built-in image tool to remove studio backgrounds while preserving the products, equal four-column / three-row grid, textures, hardware and colors. Prompt: remove gray backgrounds, floors, grid lines and cast shadows; use true transparency with clean alpha silhouettes and open handle/strap gaps; retain all twelve unbranded products and equal cell positions; no text, logos or extra objects. Individual PNG tiles are shipped as product-1.png through product-12.png. JPEG masters remain for compatibility.

The theme thumbnail demo-preview.webp is an optimized 960 × 600 capture of the actual Sable storefront, including navigation, headline and circular collections.
