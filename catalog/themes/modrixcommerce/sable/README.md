# Sable Atelier

An original travel goods theme inspired by the editorial pacing of the supplied reference: an overlay header, cinematic hero, circular collections, studio campaign, four-column product edits, landscape campaigns, brand story and quiet footer. All wordmarks, copy and campaign/product artwork are original to this package.

## Customize

Open Admin → Appearance → Themes → Sable Atelier → Customize. This package uses the platform's current section editor (sections v2): a live preview, desktop/mobile sizes, selectable sections and blocks, reorder/add/remove controls, drafts, Save and Publish. Edit Home, Product, Collection, Search, Cart and Page templates and shared Header/Footer groups. Section settings contain all text, imagery, links, collection sources and layouts. Theme settings contain shared colors, typography and the product image surface.

The header has editable menu-link blocks and an optional home overlay. Image banners support separate mobile artwork, shading, alignment, height and inset width. Product grids support three/four desktop columns, limits, quick add and actual product color swatches. Phones use two columns and a horizontally scrollable collection rail.

## Commerce and content

Products, stock, prices, currency, sale labels and cart actions use platform services. A single purchasable variant adds to the bag; products with options open their product page. Color swatches reflect configured variant colors and open the product options. Product details, quantity, variants, stock errors, shipping, checkout, account and recovery pages retain platform behavior.

The newsletter section renders the real Newsletter extension at the storefront.footer placement when enabled. Enable and place that block in the extension settings to show its working consent/signup form. No substitute form collects or discards email.

Optional demo content provides 12 products and three collections. Import it explicitly in Themes to populate a demonstration store, then choose collections in each product grid. It is never imported automatically and existing merchant products are preserved. Replace demo descriptions, prices and stock before selling.

## Reference page layouts (1.1)

The product template has a portrait gallery, compact purchase panel, actual color options, quantity controls, expandable product information and a five-column recommendation row. Information panels are editable blocks. The catalog uses four portrait cards per row on desktop, two on mobile, small Availability and Price popovers, sorting, product counts and numbered pagination. The collection template begins with an editable landscape campaign and a pair of featured collection cards; arrow controls advance through the tile blocks.

The footer includes editable contact fields, column links, optional social profiles and accepted-payment marks, plus the store language selector. Payment marks and provider promotions are shown only when configured. Free service add-ons from the existing Product Addons module appear as compact gift options and retain their normal cart behavior. Paid add-ons keep the module interface.

Product cutouts are available as assets/product-1.png through assets/product-12.png. The original JPEG assets remain compatible with saved image settings. The theme-manager thumbnail is assets/demo-preview.webp, captured from the storefront at 1600 × 1000 and optimized to 960 × 600.
