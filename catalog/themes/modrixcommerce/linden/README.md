# Linden & Vale

A botanical beauty storefront theme with an editorial home page, filterable
catalog, gallery-led product page, and mobile navigation.

Open **Appearance → Themes → Linden & Vale → Customize**. The editor lets you
reorder sections and edit imagery, copy, calls to action, navigation, footer
groups, colors, fonts, type sizes, and card proportions. The Header section
controls sticky navigation. The Product section controls the gallery, detail
accordions, recommendations, and the optional Buy now button.

Use the product and category editors to maintain the underlying catalog,
including names, images, prices, inventory, sizes, and variants. Use
**Import demo content** to populate a new preview with the sample catalog and
campaigns. Work Sans and Fraunces ship with their font licenses in `assets`.
The sample imagery was created for Linden & Vale. Its prompts are recorded in
`assets/image-prompts.json`.

The theme renders the platform’s standard storefront placement slots. Enable
and configure extensions in the admin, then select their storefront
placements. Product placements include the price area, purchase area, and
description. Cart, checkout, global overlays, and account pages use the shared
storefront layout and commerce pipeline. Payment methods, installment
messages, rewards, and discount calculations come from the configured modules.
