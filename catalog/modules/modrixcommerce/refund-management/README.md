# Advanced Refund Management

Adds advanced refund management to Modrix Commerce, enabling merchants to process partial and full refunds, generate credit memos, and track refund payments directly from the Admin Panel (`Admin → Sales → Refunds & Credit Memos`).

## Capabilities

- **Partial & Full Refunds**: Line-item refund calculator with per-item restock quantities, shipping refund, adjustments, full-balance refunds, and custom amount refunds.
- **Credit Memos**: Sequential, immutable credit memos (`CM-{year}-00001`) issued automatically on refund or on demand, with downloadable PDF documents.
- **Refund Payment Tracking**: Track payout channel (payment gateway, bank transfer, cash, card terminal, store credit, check), settlement status, reference IDs, and recipient details.
