# Delivery Date & Time Slots for Modrix

Give your customers the flexibility to schedule their preferred delivery date and time slot during checkout, while empowering store operations with capacity limits, lead times, cutoff rules, and blackout date management.

## Features

- **Checkout Delivery Picker**: Shoppers can pick an available delivery date and choose from active time windows (e.g., Morning 9 AM – 1 PM, Afternoon 1 PM – 5 PM).
- **Daily Capacity Limits**: Cap orders per slot per day to prevent delivery fleet bottlenecks. Fully booked slots automatically show as sold out.
- **Cutoff Time Protection**: Configure lead time (in hours) and same-day cutoffs (e.g., no same-day bookings after 2:00 PM).
- **Blackout Dates & Holidays**: Easily block national holidays, warehouse closures, or maintenance days.
- **Delivery Instructions**: Let customers provide gate codes, apartment numbers, or specific driver instructions.
- **Admin Delivery Schedule**: Full calendar / schedule view of all orders grouped by date and slot, with quick filters and customer contact details.
- **Order Tracking Integration**: Scheduled delivery info automatically displays on the customer's order confirmation page and order history.
