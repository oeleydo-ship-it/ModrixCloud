# Store Pickup & Click & Collect for Modrix

Give your customers the flexibility to pick up their orders in-store or at local retail branch locations instead of waiting for home delivery, with multi-location management, preparation workflow, alternative collector authorization, and ready notifications.

## Features

- **Checkout In-Store Pickup Selector**: Shoppers can choose in-store pickup directly as a shipping method and select their nearest branch location with operating hours and estimated preparation lead times.
- **Multi-Branch Management**: Add, update, and manage multiple physical stores, retail outlets, and pickup points with custom addresses, phone numbers, operating hours, and directions.
- **Alternative Collector Authorization**: Allows shoppers to designate another person (with name and phone) authorized to collect their order.
- **Preferred Pickup Scheduling**: Optional date and time window preferences so customers can indicate when they plan to visit the store.
- **Order Preparation & Readiness Tracking**: Back-office order dashboard with status workflow (`Preparing Order` → `Ready for Pickup` → `Picked Up / Completed`) and order event logging.
- **Order Confirmation & Account Receipts**: Detailed pickup card on the customer's order confirmation and account order pages with branch address, hours, collector name, and ready status.
- **Flexible Pricing**: Configure free in-store pickup or optional handling fees per branch.
